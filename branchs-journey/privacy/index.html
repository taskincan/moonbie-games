Privacy Policy — The Branch's Journey

Moonbie Games · Last updated: 10 October 2026

The Branch's Journey (Dalın Yolculuğu, "the game") is a fully offline platformer by Moonbie Games.

  • No personal data is collected. The game does not collect, store or share names, e-mail addresses, location, device or advertising identifiers, or any other personal data.
  • No internet access. The game requests no internet permission and connects to no server.
  • No ads, analytics or third-party services. There are no ad networks, analytics SDKs, sign-in or in-app purchases.
  • Data kept on your device: only the game settings (language, volume, touch button size) and your progress, stored locally on your device. They never leave it and are removed when you uninstall the game.
  • Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.
  • Changes: any update to this policy will be published on this page.
  • Contact: moonbiegames@gmail.com
