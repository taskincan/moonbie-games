Privacy Policy — The Branch's Journey

Moonbie Games · Last updated: 11 October 2026

The Branch's Journey (Dalın Yolculuğu, Die Reise des Zweigs; "the game") is a platformer by Moonbie Games for Android, iPhone/iPad and desktop. Spring is free to play; the full game is a one-time in-app purchase.

  • No personal data is collected. The game does not collect, store or share names, e-mail addresses, location, device or advertising identifiers, or any other personal data.
  • Plays offline. The game itself connects to no server of ours; you can play without an internet connection.
  • One optional purchase, handled by the store. Spring is free; the rest of the game can be unlocked with a single in-app purchase through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone/iPad). Payment is processed entirely by Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy) or Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) under their privacy policies. We never receive your card or payment details; the store gives us only basic order information (such as the order number, country and price) for accounting and refunds, and the game itself only learns whether the full game is owned. On Android, Google's Play Billing library (which adds the internet and network-state permissions) talks to the Google Play app on your device; on iPhone/iPad, Apple's StoreKit does the same.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. There are no ad networks, analytics SDKs, sign-in or tracking.
  • Data kept on your device: only the game settings (language, volume, touch button size), your progress and whether the full game is unlocked, stored locally on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game; the purchase itself can be restored from the store.
  • Children: we collect no data from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, where parents can require approval for purchases.
  • Changes: any update to this policy will be published on this page.
  • Contact: moonbiegames@gmail.com

Datenschutzerklärung — Die Reise des Zweigs

Moonbie Games · Zuletzt aktualisiert: 11. Oktober 2026

Die Zahlung wird vollständig von Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy) bzw. Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) gemäß deren Datenschutzbestimmungen abgewickelt. Bestellnummer, Land und Preis) für Buchhaltung und Erstattungen, und das Spiel erfährt nur, ob die Vollversion gekauft wurde.
